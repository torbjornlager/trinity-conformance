Source-copying repairs and ACTOR release verification — 1 November 2026
===================================================================

The five-case source-copying-0.1 pack reproduces three Trealla boundaries missed
by the earlier separate source and guard packs. On the unchanged Trealla base,
SCOPY-001 fails with permission_error for sandbox_builtin_call(A=a), SCOPY-003
fails with no_next, and SCOPY-005 fails when its copied timeout callback becomes
an invalid []/0 call. Both named controls, SCOPY-002 and SCOPY-004, pass.
The repaired Trealla candidate passes all five, as does the SWI candidate.

Mechanisms repaired
-------------------
- Exporting a private predicate restores the public goal from the internal
  sandbox_builtin_call wrapper; clause/2's corresponding restoration agrees.
  This changes source restoration, not the admission policy for submitted
  module-qualified goals.
- An inline child entry goal remains unqualified until the child's namespace
  exists. Qualifying its receive in the copied parent's namespace made the
  child retain execution references to the wrong private environment.
- Option lists are reconstructed structurally during source restoration.
  Generic compound reconstruction corrupted copied receive timeout callbacks;
  the list clause preserves their ordinary list structure.

SCOPY-003 explicitly supplies the inline child's sc_value/1 generator through
src_predicates/1. Without private child source, an inline builtin-only worker
is not a discriminating negative control and can pass on the old source.
Worker and controller normal termination are both observed before passing.

Release gate
------------
The versioned actor-release-0.1 gate runs seven named packs and requires all
expected case IDs, exactly once, all passing. Missing reports, unsupported
cases, duplicates, failures or nonzero subprocess exits prevent success.
The default actor-core runner is unchanged for backward compatibility.

The final stable run passes 40/40 on SWI and 40/40 on Trealla (80/80 total):
actor core 7; selective receive 6; links/monitors 6; copying/isolation 6;
source copying 5; toplevel waits 6; ownership/lifecycle 4.

The local adapter provisions two distinct execution-only identities on each
private loopback fixture, runs the gate and removes its own node processes.
The administrator exception matters: a node owner may legitimately exit
another connection's actors. An authenticated owner-browser probe cannot
establish a missing denial. The correct OL-001 execution-only probe passes
on both tested candidates. OL-003/004 preserve computing-child/grandchild
monitor notifications across owning-socket EOF on both candidates.
